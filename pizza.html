Delicious Pizza

Recipe

To make chicken fajita pizza, start by preparing the chicken. Cut the chicken into small bite-sized pieces and heat some cooking oil in a pan. Add the chicken and season it with salt, black pepper, chili powder, paprika, and garlic. Cook the chicken over medium heat until it is completely cooked and lightly browned. Then add sliced onion and bell pepper (capsicum) and cook them with the chicken until they become slightly soft while still keeping some of their crunch. Once the chicken and vegetables are ready, place the pizza base on a baking tray and spread an even layer of pizza sauce over the top. Make sure the sauce covers most of the base without adding too much, so the pizza does not become soggy.

Next, spread the cooked chicken, onion, and bell pepper evenly over the pizza sauce. Cover the toppings generously with shredded mozzarella cheese. Sprinkle oregano and chili flakes over the cheese for extra flavor. Preheat the oven to 200°C (400°F) and place the pizza inside. Bake it for about 12–15 minutes, or until the mozzarella cheese has completely melted and the edges of the pizza base become golden and slightly crispy. When the pizza is ready, carefully remove it from the oven and let it rest for a couple of minutes so the cheese can settle. Finally, cut the chicken fajita pizza into slices and serve it hot with your favorite dip or sauce.

Ingredients for Chicken Fajita Pizza

  • Pizza base
  • Chicken
  • Pizza sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Onion
  • Bell pepper (capsicum)
  • Cooking oil
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Chili powder
  • Paprika
  • Garlic
  • Oregano
  • Chili flakes

Step By Step Recipe

  1. Cooking oil + chicken → cook the chicken with salt, black pepper, chili powder, paprika, and garlic.
  2. Onion + bell pepper → add and cook with the chicken.
  3. Pizza base → place it on the tray
  4. Pizza sauce → spread it on the base.
  5. Cooked chicken and vegetables → add them on top.
  6. Mozzarella cheese → add on top.
  7. Oregano + chili flakes → sprinkle over the cheese.
  8. Bake → until the cheese melts and the crust is golden.
